## Findings
- MINOR: `client/src/screens/Player/SlideImage.module.css:51` still computes the outgoing drift as `calc(var(--slide-out-scale-to) / var(--slide-out-scale-from))` with the fixed `1.06` divisor in `client/src/styles/tokens.css:123`, while the Ken Burns end scale now varies from 1.02 to 1.10. The composition arithmetic documented in `tasks/T032-player-animations.md:364` (outgoing image ends at exactly 1.10) therefore only holds for a 10-14 s duration: at 1 s the outgoing layer ends at ~1.058 and at 30 s at ~1.141. Either divide by the live Ken Burns target (`calc(var(--slide-out-scale-to) / var(--kenburns-scale))`) or state the deviation explicitly so the crossfade drift does not overshoot spec §9.4's 1.10.
- MINOR: `client/test/styles/tokens.test.ts:257` pins the fallback token to the literal `1.04` while the mapping lives in `client/src/screens/Player/motion.ts:42-49`; nothing asserts the two agree.
